An electrical engineering internship at an accredited training company

AET Solutions is an SBB-accredited training company. Studying electrical engineering and looking for an internship where you'll do real work? With us, you'll work on real jobs and learn the trade in practice, across the Utrecht, Amersfoort and Amsterdam region.

Apply for an internship

What you'll learn

  • Meter cupboards and distribution boards

    Replacing and extending fuse boxes in homes and commercial premises.

  • EV chargers

    Installing a dedicated circuit, running the cable, and mounting and configuring the charger.

  • Testing and completion

    Testing an installation, checking RCDs and signing off the work.

  • Electrical work in renovations

    Installing and finishing cabling and connection points.

What your internship looks like

You join us on the jobs we carry out, so you see each job from start to finish: the assessment, the preparation, the installation, and the testing on completion. You work to NEN 1010 and learn to work safely in accordance with NEN 3140. We build up what you do independently step by step.

What we ask of you

  • You are following a vocational (mbo) course in electrical engineering.
  • You are motivated and reliable.
  • You work carefully and take safety seriously.

How to apply

Use the form to send us:

  • Your course, level and year.
  • Whether you follow the BOL (school-based) or BBL (work-based) route.
  • The period and number of days per week.
  • A few sentences on why you would like to do your internship with us.

Please email your CV to info@aet-solutions.nl. We will respond to every application and let you know whether a place is available at that time. Apply now.
