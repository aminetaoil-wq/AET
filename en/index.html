Standards

Safe work to recognised standards

Two standards govern how we work: how an installation must be built, and how to work on it safely.

NEN 3140

The standard for working safely on, and managing, electrical installations. All work at AET Solutions is carried out by an experienced, qualified NEN 3140 Skilled Person (Vakbekwaam Persoon).

NEN 1010

The standard setting out the safety requirements for designing and installing low-voltage installations, such as the wiring in your home or business premises. Every installation is built to NEN 1010 and fully tested before sign-off.

FAQ

Good to know before you start

Is my meter cupboard ready for an EV charger, induction hob or home battery?

That depends on your supply (single-phase or three-phase), the rating of your main fuse and the space left in your fuse box. Each high-power appliance needs its own circuit. Send us a photo of your meter cupboard and we'll give you an initial assessment. See also our fuse box page.

How much does an electrician cost?

It varies from job to job. The price is made up of materials, labour and any travel costs. Most of it depends on the number of circuits or connection points, the length of the cable runs and any changes to your fuse box. You'll receive a no-obligation quote with a fixed price before any work starts.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes. AET Solutions guarantees the work carried out for 12 months from completion: any defects resulting from our workmanship are put right free of charge. Materials such as fuse boxes, circuit breakers, EV chargers and home batteries are covered by the manufacturer's warranty, and we'll handle any claim for you. Full details are in our terms and conditions.

What happens if extra work is needed?

If anything unexpected comes up during the work, we discuss it with you first. We never carry out extra work without your approval.

Can you call me instead?

Of course. Leave your name, phone number and preferred time via a callback request and we'll call you back.

Which areas do you cover?

For residential clients: Utrecht, Amersfoort, Amsterdam, Nieuwegein, Zeist, Houten, Veenendaal, Hilversum, Woerden and the surrounding areas. For businesses: nationwide, on a project basis.

What qualifications does AET Solutions have?

All work at AET Solutions is carried out by an experienced, qualified NEN 3140 Skilled Person (Vakbekwaam Persoon). Every installation is built to NEN 1010, the Dutch standard for electrical installations, and fully tested on completion.

Do you work as a subcontractor?

Yes, for installation companies and for suppliers of home batteries and EV chargers, per job or per project. See Business.
