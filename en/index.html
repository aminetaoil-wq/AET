Standards

Safe work to the applicable standards

Two standards determine how we work: how an installation must be built, and how to work on it safely.

NEN 3140

The standard for working safely on and managing electrical installations. The work of AET Solutions is carried out by a qualified and experienced NEN 3140 Qualified Person (Vakbekwaam Persoon).

NEN 1010

The standard with the safety requirements for designing and installing low-voltage installations, such as the wiring in your home or commercial building. Every installation is carried out to NEN 1010 and tested before handover.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know in advance

Is my meter cupboard ready for an EV charger, induction hob or home battery?

That depends on your connection (single-phase or three-phase), the rating of your main fuse and the free space in your fuse box. Appliances with a high power rating each get their own circuit. Send a photo of your meter cupboard and you will receive a first assessment. More on the fuse box page.

What does an electrician cost?

That differs per job. The price consists of materials, labour and any travel costs. The number of circuits or connection points, the length of the cables and changes to your fuse box make up most of it. You receive a no-obligation quote with a fixed price in advance.

Do you give a guarantee?

Yes. AET Solutions gives a 12-month guarantee on the work carried out, from handover: defects caused by the workmanship are put right free of charge. On materials such as fuse boxes, circuit breakers, EV chargers and home batteries, the manufacturer's guarantee applies; we arrange it for you if something is wrong. The full conditions are in our terms and conditions.

What happens if extra work is needed?

If something unexpected comes up during the work, we discuss it with you first. We only carry out extra work with your approval.

Can you call me instead of me calling you?

Yes. Leave your name, phone number and preferred time via a callback request, and we will call you back.

Which area do you cover?

For private customers: Utrecht, Amersfoort, Amsterdam, Nieuwegein, Zeist, Houten, Veenendaal, Hilversum, Woerden and surrounding towns. Business: nationwide, per project.

What qualifications does AET Solutions have?

The work of AET Solutions is carried out by a qualified and experienced NEN 3140 Qualified Person (Vakbekwaam Persoon). Every installation is carried out to NEN 1010, the standard for electrical installations in the Netherlands. At handover we measure and test everything.

Do you also work as a subcontractor?

Yes, for installation companies and for suppliers of home batteries and EV chargers, per job or per project. See Business.
