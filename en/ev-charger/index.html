EV charger installation in Utrecht, Amersfoort and Amsterdam

An EV charger puts a heavy load on your installation for hours at a time. That is why we connect it to a dedicated circuit with the right protection and, where needed, load balancing, so the main fuse doesn't trip when you cook and charge at the same time.

Book an appointmentRequest a callback

When do you need this?

  • You drive, or are about to drive, an electric car and have your own driveway or parking space.
  • You currently charge from a socket using the portable charger.
  • You have bought a charger and need someone to install it.
  • Your main fuse trips while you are charging.

Our approach

  1. AssessmentWe check your supply, the space in the fuse box and the route to the charging point.
  2. Circuit and cableWe install a dedicated circuit with the matching protection and run the cable to the charging point.
  3. InstallationWe mount and configure the charger, then verify the connection with a test.
  4. HandoverWe show you how to charge, how the app works and how to operate the charger.

Single-phase or three-phase: how fast can you charge?

  • Single-phase, 16 Aapprox. 3.7 kW
  • Single-phase, 32 Aapprox. 7.4 kW
  • Three-phase, 16 Aapprox. 11 kW

The actual speed also depends on your car's on-board charger. Want to charge at 11 kW but have a single-phase supply? See three-phase preparation.

Load balancing

A meter in your meter cupboard monitors your total consumption. If demand gets too high, the charger automatically reduces the charging current. This prevents the main fuse from tripping and often avoids the need for a heavier connection.

Apartments and rented homes

You will need permission from your owners' association (VvE) or landlord. Arrange this in advance to avoid delays.

What's included

  • Dedicated circuit with circuit breaker and suitable RCD protection
  • Cable from the meter cupboard to the charging point
  • Mounting and configuration of the charger
  • Testing and a full explanation on completion

What determines the price?

  • The charger itself, if you buy it through us
  • Length and route of the cable, including any excavation
  • Changes to the fuse box
  • Load balancing

You'll receive a no-obligation quote with a fixed price up front, with each of these items listed separately. No extra work is carried out without your approval.

Safety and standards

An EV charger needs RCD protection that also responds to DC leakage. If this is not built into the charger, we fit it in the fuse box.

Every installation is built to NEN 1010 and fully tested before sign-off. All work is carried out by an experienced, qualified NEN 3140 Skilled Person (Vakbekwaam Persoon). More about our standards and working methods.

Frequently asked questions

Can't I just charge from a standard socket?

Occasionally, at around 2.3 kW. But a standard socket is not designed to run at full load for hours, day after day.

Can you install a charger I have bought myself?

Yes. Let us know the make and model, and we will determine the protection and cabling it needs.

Do I need three-phase?

Only if you want to charge at 11 kW. If you mostly charge overnight, single-phase is often sufficient.

Can the charger run on solar power?

Many chargers can charge from your surplus solar power. Whether that works for you depends on the model and the metering in your meter cupboard.

Can my employer reimburse the electricity?

Yes, with a charger that records your consumption. See business reimbursement.
