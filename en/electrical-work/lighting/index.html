Lighting installation in homes and commercial buildings

Good lighting makes a room usable: enough light where you work, atmosphere where you relax. We install light points, recessed spotlights, dimmers and switches, neatly concealed and matched to the lamps you choose.

Book an appointmentCall me back

When do you need this?

  • You want recessed spotlights in a ceiling or kitchen.
  • A room has only one light point in the middle.
  • You want to dim lighting or switch it from two places.
  • Existing dimmers flicker or hum with LED lamps.

How we go about it

  1. Lighting planWe discuss which rooms need which light and where you want to switch it.
  2. InstallationWe run cables to the light points and switches, through existing conduits or neatly concealed.
  3. FittingWe fit spotlights, light fittings and switches.
  4. TestingWe test all switching and dimmers with the lamps that will be used.

Dimming with LED lamps

Not every dimmer works with every LED lamp. A wrong combination causes flickering, humming or lamps that do not switch off completely. We choose a dimmer that suits the lamps and the number of spotlights.

Bathrooms and damp rooms

In a bathroom, utility room or under a canopy, we choose light fittings with an ingress protection rating that suits the location, and take into account the zones around the shower and bath.

A lighting plan per room

A living room benefits from several light sources that you can switch or dim separately: general light, light over the dining table and mood lighting. In a kitchen you mainly want good light on the worktop, for example under the wall cabinets. In a hall or on the stairs, switching from two places is convenient. We think along about the position of light points and switches, so the lighting suits how you use the room.

What is included?

  • Cables to light points and switches
  • Fitting spotlights, light fittings and switches
  • Dimmers matched to the lamps
  • Testing all switching

What does the price depend on?

  • The number of light points and spotlights
  • The route of the cables
  • Cut-outs in the ceiling or wall
  • Any travel costs

You receive a no-obligation quote with a fixed price in advance, with these items listed separately. Extra work only with your approval.

Safety and standards

Recessed spotlights in a ceiling need space and ventilation; we pay attention to the distance from insulation and wooden joists.

Every installation is carried out to NEN 1010 and tested before handover. The work is carried out by a qualified and experienced NEN 3140 Qualified Person (Vakbekwaam Persoon). More about our standards and how we work.

Frequently asked questions

Do you also supply the lamps?

We can, but you do not have to use us for that. If you choose your own light fittings, we connect them and check that they match the dimmer.

Can spotlights go in any ceiling?

In a suspended ceiling, usually yes. In a concrete ceiling it is more work; we check this beforehand.

Can I switch from two places?

Yes, with two-way switching, for example at the bottom and top of the stairs.

Do you also install lighting in commercial buildings?

Yes, for offices and business premises. See also the Business page.
