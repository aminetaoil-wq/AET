Garden lighting and outdoor sockets

Lights along the path, spotlights under the trees or a socket by the terrace. Outdoor installations call for different materials than indoors: cable rated for burial, weatherproof fittings and dedicated protection. We handle the whole job, from the meter cupboard to the last light.

Book an appointmentRequest a callback

When do you need this?

  • You are landscaping a new garden or relaying the paving.
  • You want lighting along a path, driveway or terrace.
  • You want an outdoor socket for garden tools or outdoor heating.
  • Your existing garden lighting keeps cutting out after rain.

Our approach

  1. PlanWe agree where the lights and sockets will go and the best route for the cable.
  2. Cable layingThe cable is buried at the correct depth, in protective ducting where needed, before paving or planting.
  3. ConnectionWe connect the fittings and sockets and install a switch, timer or dusk sensor.
  4. TestingWe test the outdoor circuit and the RCD protection.

Plan it before you pave

Ideally the cable goes in before the garden is finished. If you are laying new paving or planting, plan the electrics at the same time. It can be done afterwards, but then the ground has to be opened up again.

Switching

Garden lighting can be operated with a standard switch, a timer or a dusk sensor that switches on automatically when it gets dark. We install whatever suits the way you use it.

Low voltage or 230 volts?

Much garden lighting runs at low voltage, 12 or 24 volts, via a transformer. It is easy to extend and the cable does not need to be buried as deep. More powerful fittings and outdoor sockets run at 230 volts and need buried cable at the proper depth. Which suits you depends on what you want to light; often a combination works best.

What's included

  • Dedicated circuit with RCD protection
  • Buried cable with protective ducting where needed
  • Connection of fittings and outdoor sockets
  • Switch, timer or dusk sensor

What determines the price?

  • Length of the cable run in the garden
  • Excavation and any lifting of paving
  • Number of lights and sockets
  • Travel costs, where applicable

You'll receive a no-obligation quote with a fixed price up front, with each of these items listed separately. No extra work is carried out without your approval.

Safety and standards

Outdoors, moisture is the biggest risk. We only use cable and fittings designed for outdoor use, and every outdoor circuit is protected by an RCD.

Every installation is built to NEN 1010 and fully tested before sign-off. All work is carried out by an experienced, qualified NEN 3140 Skilled Person (Vakbekwaam Persoon). More about our standards and working methods.

Frequently asked questions

Can I dig the trench myself?

Yes, and it will save you money. We will tell you the depth and route beforehand and check the trench before the cable goes in.

Can I use plug-in garden lights?

For temporary use, yes. For permanent lighting, a fixed connection is safer and neater.

Why does my garden lighting cut out after rain?

Usually because moisture gets into a fitting, joint or socket. In a new installation we prevent this by using the right materials.

Can an outdoor socket be locked?

Yes, there are sockets with a lockable cover.
