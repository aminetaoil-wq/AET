About AET Solutions

AET Solutions carries out electrical installations for residential clients and works as a subcontractor for the trade. Below you can read how we work, what to expect on completion and what qualifications we hold.

What we do

Electrical installation work, meter cupboards and distribution boards, EV chargers and complete home battery systems. We specialise in installations that place higher demands on the supply: charging infrastructure, energy storage and the extension of distribution boards.

For residential clients, we take care of everything from advice to completion. For installation companies and suppliers, we provide capacity as a subcontractor. See our Business page for more.

How we work

  • A quote with a fixed scope and fixed price before we start.
  • Extra work only after consultation and with your approval.
  • A single point of contact from enquiry to completion.
  • All work carried out to NEN 1010.
  • Every installation is fully tested on completion, with an explanation of how to use it.

Qualifications

  • Experienced, qualified NEN 3140 Skilled Person.
  • Every installation built to NEN 1010.
  • SBB-accredited training company: we train electrical engineering students on the job. More about internships.

Experience

Experience with distribution boards, EV charger installations, home batteries, lighting and switchgear.

Service area

Residential: Utrecht, Amersfoort, Amsterdam and surrounding areas. Business: nationwide, on a project basis.
